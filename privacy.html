179 Golf privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

179 Golf is published by Sledgehammer Software Ltd, 138 Walter Road, Swansea SA1 5RQ, United Kingdom.

The short version

179 Golf does not collect anything about you. It has no account, no sign-up and no adverts, and it makes no network connection of any kind. Nothing you enter leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

What the app stores

Scores, playing handicaps, side pot results and your settings are written to storage on your own device. They stay there. They are not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. Deleting the app deletes that data.

What the app sends

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and no third party software development kits that transmit data. It does not read your contacts, your photos, your location or your calendar.

Sharing scores between phones

The share code is a short piece of text the app generates for you. You choose whether to send it and who to send it to, using whatever messaging app you prefer. The code travels through that app, not through us. We never see it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anybody, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
