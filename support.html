179Golf

179 Golf keeps the score for a golf trip. Team formats, net scoring off your own handicap, full cards for the whole field, and it all works with the phone in aeroplane mode.

Support

Email hello@sledgehammersoftware.com and you will get a reply the same working day.

Useful things to include: your phone and iOS or Android version, which round and which screen, and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • Scores on two phones do not match. Open Share, read out the code from the phone that has the holes, and paste it into the other. Cards merge, and gaps are filled without overwriting anything already entered.
  • No signal on the course. That is fine. The app holds everything on the phone and never needs a connection.
  • Wrong shots given. Check the handicap allowance in More, and check the stroke index on the course card against the real scorecard on the first tee.
  • Scores gone. Scores live on the phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Privacy

The app collects nothing and makes no network connection. Full policy: 179 Golf privacy policy.
